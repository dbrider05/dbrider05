### 🔍 About Me
I work on power semiconductor devices, focusing on Si/SiC power modules, power module design, and advanced packaging.

I currently design and develop IGBT power modules for industrial applications, meeting specified voltage and current ratings while balancing electrical, thermal, mechanical, and reliability requirements.

My work involves analysing and evaluating key aspects of power module construction, including bare-die design and fabrication, chip-near and chip-remote interconnections, die-attach technologies, substrate materials, and wire-bonding techniques. I am particularly interested in how material selection, interconnection design, and packaging architecture affect overall module performance and reliability.

In addition to design, I am developing expertise in power module validation, device characterization, thermal and electrical analysis, and reliability testing. I am interested in understanding the end-to-end process, from semiconductor die to a reliable, manufacturable power module.

My broader interests include power semiconductors, SiC/GaN devices, advanced packaging, thermal management, and power module reliability.

I welcome opportunities to connect with professionals working in power semiconductor devices, power module design and packaging, and advanced semiconductor packaging.
